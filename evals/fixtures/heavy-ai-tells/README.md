## How it works

The system is initiated by the orchestrator, after which jobs are scheduled by the scheduler component, and then data is processed by the workers, and finally results are stored by the storage layer. It's not uncommon for users to not find the configuration unfriendly, and the documentation isn't unhelpful either.

When a job is submitted, what happens is that the orchestrator, which is the central coordinator of the entire DataFlow Pro distributed system that manages all of the various worker nodes that are deployed across the cluster, decides how to schedule it.
